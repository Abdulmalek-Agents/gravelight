# ⚠️ Gravelight — Deprecation Notice

## Status
**Halted as of 2026-05-22.** No new development planned.

## Why

Gravelight was conceived and approved as a low-budget, ~9-month, **2D** indie ship in the bullet-heaven / survivors-like lane. The 14-expert board scored it 8.21/10 (2nd-highest of 6 candidates).

After founder review, the studio direction was clarified: **all Inventix titles will be 3D-native** to align with the studio's 3D-first portfolio strategy.

A forced 2D → 3D port for Gravelight would:
- Break the genre's core technical contract (500+ entities on screen at 60fps — only viable in 2D with sprite batching)
- Lose the lavender-and-moss pixel-art identity that was Elena's primary differentiator
- Drive the team's cost from ~$20k (2D shippable) to ~$400k (forced 3D port) for a comparable scope
- Change the genre from "bullet heaven" (a $250M+/yr lane) to "3D action roguelite" (a different and more competitive lane)

The expanded 18-expert board reviewed the situation and **unanimously voted to deprecate rather than port**. The 2D design is preserved here for historical reference. The cozy-necromancy theme may resurface in a future 3D concept.

## What replaces it

The board selected two 3D-native concepts as Gravelight's portfolio replacements:

| Replacement | Genre | Repo |
|---|---|---|
| 🪡 Aetherloom | 3D 2-player co-op action roguelite (Hades × Children of Morta) | [aetherloom](https://github.com/Abdulmalek-Agents/aetherloom) |
| 🦋 Lumen Isles | 3D cozy photography exploration (Pokémon Snap × Eastshade) | [lumen-isles](https://github.com/Abdulmalek-Agents/lumen-isles) |
