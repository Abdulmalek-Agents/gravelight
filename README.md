# 🕯️ Gravelight — ⚠️ DEPRECATED (2D concept)

> **This 2D concept has been halted.** Studio direction shifted to 3D-native projects with bigger teams and 2-year ship windows. See [`DEPRECATED.md`](DEPRECATED.md) for full rationale.

## What happened

Gravelight was greenlit by the 14-expert Portfolio Review Board in May 2026 as a low-budget, 9-month 2D bullet-heaven shippable concept (8.21/10 average). After founder review, the studio committed to **3D-native projects only**. The bullet-heaven genre is structurally 2D (500+ entities at 60fps relies on 2D sprite batching) and does not port cleanly to 3D.

## What's still here

- 14-expert board approval audit trail
- Performance plan, marketing plan, asset plan

Feel free to fork as a historical reference. **No new development is planned here.**
