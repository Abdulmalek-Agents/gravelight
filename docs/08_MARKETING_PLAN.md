# 📣 Marketing Plan — Gravelight

> Audience capture flows from Vampire Survivors / Brotato / Halls of Torment fans → plus cozy crossover from Spiritfarer / Cult of the Lamb fans. Two distinct funnels, one game.

## 1. Target audience profile

### Primary: Bullet-heaven core
- 18–40 yo, plays Steam 3–20 hrs/week
- Owns Vampire Survivors, Brotato, Halls of Torment, 20 Minutes Till Dawn
- Watches Olexa, Retromation, Splattercatgaming
- Buys games <$15 on impulse

### Secondary: Cozy-narrative
- 24–45 yo, plays Steam 1–5 hrs/week
- Owns Spiritfarer, Cult of the Lamb, Stardew, Coffee Talk
- Follows /r/CozyGamers
- Lured by aesthetic and tone (the lavender capsule)

## 2. Wishlist funnel

| Phase | Wishlist gate | Action if missed |

## 6. TikTok / shorts strategy (Sasha)

- 2 shorts/week from Phase 3 onward (bullet heaven generates more content than detective)
- Highest-performing formats (from genre research):

  - 12-sec full necro-army parade at dawn
  - 22-sec narrated "how I built this run" upgrade-pick walkthrough
- Music: chiptune / lo-fi pixel-game playlists
