## 2. Primary evidence

### 2.1 Genre commercial validation
- **Vampire Survivors** (poncle, 2022): >$100M lifetime, ~$5 base price, mobile + Steam + console. Genre-defining.
- **Brotato** (Blobfish, 2023): 4M+ units, $4.99, ~96% positive.

- **Death Must Die** (Realm Archive, 2024): 800k+ in EA.
- **20 Minutes Till Dawn** (flanne, 2022): 2M+, $4.99.

### 2.2 The cozy-coded gap

Every successful entry in the genre trades on one of: blood-and-violence (VS), comedic comedy (Brotato), dark-fantasy (Halls of Torment), or horror (Death Must Die). **There is no cozy-aesthetic, kind-protagonist bullet heaven.** Gravelight occupies this exact gap:
- Lavender-and-moss palette (NOT grimdark)
- Kind young necromancer (NOT a vengeful slayer)
- Death-as-rest framing (NOT death-as-violence)
- Mourners at day, restless dead at night (cozy-horror hybrid)
